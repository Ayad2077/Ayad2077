## Featured Projects

### [AMD Equity Research & Valuation Dashboard](https://github.com/Ayad2077/amd-equity-dashboard)

An interactive, full-cycle equity research project covering AMD's financial performance, competitive position, forecasts, valuation, and investment recommendation.

**What it includes:**

- Three-statement financial analysis and ratio trends
- Revenue, margin, and segment forecasting
- Discounted cash flow valuation using CAPM-based WACC
- Trading comparables, precedent transactions, and a football field
- Bear, base, and bull scenarios with sensitivity analysis
- Interactive HTML, CSS, JavaScript, and Chart.js visualizations

**Investment conclusion:** BUY recommendation with a **$425 price target** based on the project's May 1, 2026 reference price.

### Foreign Exchange Hedging Analysis — Porsche

A corporate treasury case analyzing Porsche's North American USD/EUR exposure.

- Modeled approximately **$2.95 billion** of sales exposure across three volume scenarios
- Compared unhedged, forward, and at-the-money put strategies
- Quantified residual exposure caused by forecast error
- Recommended forwards for expected sales and out-of-the-money puts for tail-risk protection
